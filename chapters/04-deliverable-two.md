## Task D2.1 — Review and Analyze Financial Statements

Using the Deliverable One spreadsheet, calculate key financial metrics
for all available years:

| Metric | Formula |
|---|---|
| Operating Surplus/Deficit | Total Revenue − Total Expenses |
| Operating Margin | Operating Surplus / Total Revenue |
| Reserve Ratio | Cash / Total Expenses |
| Liability Burden | Liabilities / Total Revenue |
| Donation Dependence | Donations / Total Revenue |

## Task D2.2 — Firehouse Visitation

Students conduct a field visit to a local firehouse — a **venue-based,
concrete experience** central to the project's experiential learning
design. The visit engages external sensory perceptions (seeing, hearing,
smelling, touch) and internal bodily awareness (movement, pace,
effort), helping students connect abstract financial concepts to real
operational realities: labor costs, productivity, staffing decisions,
and more are all shaped by the physical realities of work.

During the visit, students should attempt to discuss:

- Equipment needing replacement (fire trucks, drones, ambulances, etc.)
- Construction or infrastructure needs
- Funding sources (donations, grants, etc., for volunteer firehouses)
- Recruitment and training needs

:::{admonition} Virtual option
:class: tip
Students with accessibility limitations may use Google Maps, Yelp, a
firehouse's website, online reviews/images, or interviews. A live
FaceTime visit shared by a classmate is also an accepted approach.
:::
