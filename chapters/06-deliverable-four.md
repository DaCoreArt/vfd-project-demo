# Deliverable Four: Communicating Results

**Course Learning Outcomes:** CLO 1 through CLO 6

Students synthesize and present the nonprofit accounting and financial
management story developed across Deliverables One, Two, and Three as a
concise, professional, narrated PowerPoint.

## Requirements

1. **Draft a storyboard** — an outline or empty slide sequence populated
   with ideas as Deliverables One through Three are completed.
2. **Record narration** — the full presentation must be **no longer than
   five minutes**. No fixed slide count is required; roughly 10 slides
   is a reasonable target.
3. **Rehearse** — timing and clarity matter for the recorded narration.
   Students with speech-related accommodation needs should consult the
   Office of Accommodations for alternatives such as closed captioning.
4. **Last slide:** References (Citations), APA 7th Edition.

:::{note} Helpful references for building a narrated presentation
- Storyboarding walkthroughs (e.g., Leila Gharani's Microsoft product
  tutorials) can help structure the presentation before recording.
- Microsoft Office Support documentation covers recording narration and
  slide timings directly in PowerPoint.
:::
