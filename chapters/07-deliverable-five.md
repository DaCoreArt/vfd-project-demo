# Deliverable Five: Reflection

**Course Learning Outcomes:** CLO 1 through CLO 6 · **Individual effort only**

## Project Learning Outcomes

1. Integrate employer-desired competencies to develop highly valued
   employer-desired skills.
2. Construct and analyze a comprehensive financial profile of a
   nonprofit organization using real-world data and field-based
   observations.
3. Analyze real nonprofit financial data and interpret nonprofit
   operations from a nonprofit accounting and financial management
   perspective.
4. Defend evidence-based financial recommendations under realistic
   operational constraints in a nonprofit context.
5. Communicate financial insights and reflect on experiential learning
   to demonstrate professional judgment, analytical thinking, and
   applied competency development.

Deliverable Five is designed to critically review how the combination of
learning nonprofit accounting and financial management concepts from the
textbook and the signature term project helps students better learn the
course learning objectives.

Deliverable Five Reflections is a two-part process to help develop oral
and written presentation competency skills of a comprehensive
experiential learning project. Deliverable Five is solely an individual
effort.

## Task D5.1 — Create an individual reflection about the Project

For Part One of Deliverable Five, students combine the reflections from
deliverables one through four tell a flowing business story about the
events you sensed during the project, including and most importantly, the
visit to the firehouse (concrete experiences) and how different theories
you studied helped you understand them (abstract conceptualizations), and
how everything you did in the Project enhanced all of your
employer-desired competencies detailed in Deliverable One. Did the
project strengthen, weaken, or make no difference to them? You need to be
very specific in your narrative. You should write passionately and put
feeling into it.

The objective of deliverable five is to critically review, in writing,
how the combination of learning accounting and finance concepts from the
textbook and the signature term project helped students better learn the
course learning objectives.

### D5.1 Task Requirements

1. Thinking back on the Concrete Experiences of D1, D2 and D3, give a
   descriptive reflection of how the firehouse setting engaged you
   sensory perceptions and why it was important in your learning process.
2. Thinking back on the Abstract Conceptualizations (the theories) in D1,
   D2 and D3, give a descriptive reflection of how they helped you
   understand and explain nonprofit accounting and financial management.
3. Thinking back on how your competencies in D1, D2 and D3, give a
   descriptive reflection of how they were enhanced. Give very specific
   examples as if you were telling a prospective employer how this
   project helps develop your employer-desired competencies.

Each student must write up and submit their own work.

## Task D5.2 — Create an oral two to three-minute VoiceThread (individual reflection) business story about the Project

For Part Two of Deliverable Five, students are to make a three-minute
VoiceThread that orally summarizes key learning points for the Signature
Term Project in the form of a concise business story. It is akin to a
YouTube video short.

### Task Requirement

Imagine that you are asked in a job interview what experience(s) best
prepared you to add value to the employer's organization. You are to
refer to the Project and describe how you analyzed a real fire department
and how it specifically enhanced your employer-desired competences that
better enabled you to help make good financial and strategic decisions.

The VoiceThread cannot exceed three minutes. You must be concise and
impactful.

In this requirement, the instructions are purposeful not to give you an
industry for a potential interview. This is one of the exact reasons why
this project was designed the way it is. It is for a moment like this that
you have to convince an employer how you learned can be applied to all
situations. You are placed in a concrete experience, you have to explain
it with theory, and further explain how you tested the theory to the
situation. This is a foundational skill to develop.

On the VoiceThread, you must rehearse this story over and over to get it
right. One day, you may have to really tell this business story to a
prospective employer. Tell the story with passion and excitement. Really
convince the prospective employer that you are worth it!
