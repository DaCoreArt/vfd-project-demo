# Deliverable Five: Reflection

**Course Learning Outcomes:** CLO 1 through CLO 6 · **Individual effort only**

Deliverable Five critically reviews how combining textbook concepts with
the signature term project supported the course learning objectives,
through a two-part written and oral reflection process.

## Task D5.1 — Written Reflection

Combine reflections from Deliverables One through Four into a flowing
business story: the concrete experiences sensed during the project
(especially the firehouse visit), the abstract theories that helped
explain them, and how the project enhanced each employer-desired
competency (strengthened, weakened, or unchanged) — with specific
examples.

Address explicitly:

1. How the firehouse setting engaged sensory perceptions and why that
   mattered for learning.
2. How the theories studied helped explain and understand nonprofit
   accounting and financial management.
3. How competencies were enhanced — framed as if explaining to a
   prospective employer.

## Task D5.2 — Oral Reflection (VoiceThread)

A **two-to-three-minute** VoiceThread business story, as if answering a
job interview question: *"What experience best prepared you to add value
to this organization?"* Reference the project and describe how analyzing
a real fire department enhanced employer-desired competencies relevant
to financial and strategic decision-making. Must be concise, rehearsed,
and delivered with energy and conviction.
