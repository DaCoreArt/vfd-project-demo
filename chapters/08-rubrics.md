Each deliverable is graded against a four-level rubric: **Exemplary
(90–100 pts)**, **Proficient (70–89 pts)**, **Developing (50–69 pts)**,
**Beginning (0–49 pts)**. Point ranges preserve a 100-point total per
deliverable.

## Deliverable One

| Criteria | Exemplary (90–100) | Proficient (70–89) | Developing (50–69) | Beginning (0–49) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Firehouse identification | Appropriate firehouse chosen with clear rationale; financial and sensory observations well integrated | Appropriate firehouse chosen with a generally clear rationale; financial and sensory observations mostly integrated | Firehouse identified, but rationale is thin or observations are only partially tied to financial context | Inappropriate firehouse or no meaningful concrete experience described |
| Tax return location | All six years/all departments located | Most years and departments located, with only minor gaps | Some tax returns located, but coverage is incomplete across years or departments | No tax returns located |
| Excel spreadsheet | Complete names and available years | Spreadsheet mostly complete; minor naming or year gaps remain | Spreadsheet started but incomplete for several entities or years | No spreadsheet created |
| Financial data extraction | All available years extracted and populated | Most available years extracted and populated with limited omissions | Partial extraction; key fields or years missing | No data extracted |
| Summary/financial story | Comprehensive, concise, coherent | Clear summary that covers the main financial story with minor gaps | Summary present but vague, incomplete, or weakly organized | No summary or story |
| Reflection | Specific, links theory/competencies/experience | Links theory, competencies, and experience with adequate specificity | Reflection is general; links to theory or competencies are weak | Minimal or absent |
| Citations | Correct APA 7th, authoritative sources only | Mostly correct APA 7th with authoritative sources; minor format errors | Citations present but incomplete, inconsistently formatted, or partly non-acceptable | Little/no citation or non-acceptable sources |

## Deliverable Three

| Criteria | Exemplary (90–100) | Proficient (70–89) | Developing (50–69) | Beginning (0–49) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Community funding scenario | Comprehensive: targets, participation assumptions, donor segmentation, feasibility | Addresses targets, assumptions, segmentation, and feasibility with only minor gaps | Scenario outlined, but key assumptions or feasibility analysis are incomplete | Incomplete/unrealistic assumptions |
| Full grant scenario | Comprehensive: eligibility, approval likelihood, timing, restrictions, impact of delay/denial | Covers eligibility, likelihood, timing, and restrictions with sound overall analysis | Partial grant analysis; missing one or more key dimensions (timing, restrictions, or impact) | Little/no analysis |
| Short-term loan scenario | Comprehensive: correct payment calculations, cash flow timing, bridge risk, contingency planning | Calculations largely correct; cash flow timing and bridge risk addressed with limited gaps | Some calculations or risk discussion present, but incomplete or partly incorrect | Missing/incorrect calculations |
| Long-term loan scenario | Accurate calculations across metrics, sound discount rate rationale | Mostly accurate calculations with a reasonable discount rate rationale | Calculations attempted, but accuracy or discount rate rationale is weak | Incomplete/incorrect calculations |
| Reflection | Specific, links theory/competencies/experience | Links theory, competencies, and experience with adequate specificity | Reflection is general; links to theory or competencies are weak | Minimal or absent |
| Citations | Correct APA 7th | Mostly correct APA 7th; minor format errors | Citations present but incomplete or inconsistently formatted | Little/no citation |

## Deliverable Four

| Criteria | Exemplary (90–100) | Proficient (70–89) | Developing (50–69) | Beginning (0–49) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 presentation integration | 2+ experiences tied to 2+ theories, decision-oriented | Experiences tied to theory with a clear decision focus; depth slightly uneven | Limited integration of experience and theory; decision focus is weak | No meaningful integration |
| D2 presentation integration | 2+ quantitative results, clear decision-focused interpretation | Quantitative results presented with generally clear interpretation | Some results shown, but interpretation is incomplete or unclear | Results missing/incorrect, no interpretation |
| D3 presentation integration | Valuation results tied to a clear recommendation | Valuation results mostly support a recommendation | Valuation results mentioned without a clear recommendation | No meaningful use of results |
| Cross-deliverable integration | Multiple theories integrated cohesively across D1–D3 | Cross-deliverable links present and mostly cohesive | Partial cross-deliverable links; cohesion is limited | No integration present |
| Narrative & timing | Clear storyline, within time limit, polished narration | Clear storyline and timing, with only minor narration issues | Storyline or timing uneven; narration needs polish | No structure, exceeds time limit, unclear narration |
| Citations | Correct APA 7th | Mostly correct APA 7th; minor format errors | Citations present but incomplete or inconsistently formatted | Little/no citation |

## Deliverable Five

| Criteria | Exemplary (90–100) | Proficient (70–89) | Developing (50–69) | Beginning (0–49) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Written reflection — learning progression | Explicitly explains D1→D2→D3→D4 progression with specific examples | Explains progression across deliverables with adequate examples | Mentions progression, but examples are thin or incomplete | Vague, minimal, or missing |
| Written reflection — competency development | Clear growth explained across all listed competencies with examples | Growth explained for most listed competencies with examples | Competency discussion is general or covers only a few competencies | No meaningful reflection |
| Oral VoiceThread | Synthesizes full project scope into a convincing, employer-focused story | Solid synthesis of the project with a clear employer-facing message | Partial synthesis; employer focus or competency connection is limited | Task summary only, no integration or competency connection |
