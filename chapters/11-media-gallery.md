consult these before a firehouse visit to know what to look for, and after,
to connect what they observed to the financial questions the deliverables
ask.

during a firehouse visit — with the department's permission — may submit
it for inclusion. See the submission note at the bottom of this page.
:::

## Virtual visitation option

Students completing the firehouse visitation virtually (see
[Deliverable Two](04-deliverable-two.md)) may use the videos above as a
substitute for in-person observation, alongside the department websites
and mapping resources listed in the
[Introduction](01-introduction.md).

:::{admonition} Submitting a video
:class: important
Recordings made at a firehouse require the department's express
permission before submission. Include with your submission:

- The department name and visit date
- Written or emailed confirmation of permission to publish
- A one-sentence description of what the footage shows

Videos are hosted on the course's video platform and embedded here —
media files are not committed to the repository directly.
:::

## Citing a video

Videos used as evidence in any deliverable must be cited in APA 7th
edition, as with any other source:

> Department Name. (2026, March 14). *Title of video* [Video]. YouTube.
> https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID
