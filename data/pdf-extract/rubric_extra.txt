Task D1.1:  
Identify all 
volunteer 
firehouses in NYC. 
 
Identifies an appropriate firehouse and 
clearly explains why; describes finance-
related observations (equipment, training 
needs); explains personal engagement in 
the location; integrates sensory and bodily 
observations to support understanding of 
operations and costs. 
Identifies an 
appropriate firehouse; 
describes basic 
financial observations 
and engagement; 
includes some sensory 
detail. 
Provides a limited 
explanation of 
firehouse choice or 
financial engagement; 
sensory observations 
are vague or weakly 
connected. 
Inappropriate 
firehouse selected or 
concrete experience 
not meaningfully 
described.
